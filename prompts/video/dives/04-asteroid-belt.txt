Cinematic drift through main asteroid belt, 2.8 AU from Sun. Not a dense swarm — vast emptiness punctuated by scattered bodies. Ceres looms largest: 940km diameter, differentiated sphere with bright Occator Crater faculae (sodium carbonate deposits). Camera tracks alongside slow tumble of irregular Vesta (525km), its massive Rheasilvia impact basin gouging southern pole. Smaller asteroids drift at millions of km distance — points of light against star field. Camera movement: slow parallax drift, depth revealed by relative motion. 4 seconds, 24fps, 16:9, desaturated grays with subtle spectral variation (C-type dark, S-type reddish, M-type metallic).
