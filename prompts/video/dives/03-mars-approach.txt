Cinematic approach to Mars, rust-red globe filling frame. Olympus Mons dominates western hemisphere — 22km tall, 600km wide caldera complex. Camera sweeps from equator toward Tharsis bulge, revealing Valles Marineris: 4,000km canyon system, 7km deep. Dust devil traces faint spirals across Amazonis Planitia. Polar cap glints white at north pole. Thin atmosphere hazes limb — butterscotch to pale blue. Camera movement: descending arc from 10,000km to 500km, slow rotation matching planetary spin. 4 seconds, 24fps, 16:9, saturated ochre palette with cool limb contrast.
