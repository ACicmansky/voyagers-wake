Cinematic approach to Jupiter system, gas giant dominating frame. Great Red Spot: 16,000km wide anticyclone, brick-red against banded cream and ochre clouds. Camera dives toward equatorial zone, cloud tops resolving into turbulent convection cells. Io transits: volcanic plumes (Pele, Prometheus) arc 300km above sulfurous surface. Europa's fractured ice shell glints — chaos terrain, cycloidal ridges hinting at subsurface ocean. Ganymede's magnetosphere carves cavity in Jupiter's field. Callisto's ancient cratered face watches from distance. Camera movement: sweeping dive from 100,000km to 10,000km, orbital parallax revealing moon dance. 4 seconds, 24fps, 16:9, rich Jovian palette — ammonia whites, ammonium hydrosulfide browns, water ice blues.
